Monocube Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Version 2 · Applies to Monocube 1.0.0 and later (iPhone, iPad, Mac)

No personal data is collected. Your save stays on your device only. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

Monocube is a puzzle game by TRN Studios (developer: Mertcan TURAN). Contact: trnstudios.support@gmail.com.

1. What the game stores

Level progress, stars, in-game coins, hints and hammers, unlocked themes and styles, achievements, statistics, settings and language choice are kept only in the game's save file on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. You can erase them in Settings → Reset progress; deleting the app deletes them too.

2. What the game does not do

  • No account, no login. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • No ads and no advertising identifier. The game does not ask for tracking permission and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • No analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDK. No game events are sent anywhere.
  • No in-app purchases in this version.
  • No servers of our own and no internet connection needed. The Daily puzzle is generated on your device from the date.
  • No chat, profiles or user-generated content.

3. Apple

The App Store may occasionally show its own rating prompt. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the game does not learn whether or how you rated it. Apple may share aggregated, anonymous statistics with us (for example installs, or crash reports from users who chose to share them with developers in their device settings).

4. Support email

If you write to us, we receive your email address and your message and use them only to answer you. We delete them when they are no longer needed for that.

5. Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If a future version changes what data is processed (for example by adding optional ads or purchases), this page will be updated before that version is released and the version number above will increase.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: trnstudios.support@gmail.com.
