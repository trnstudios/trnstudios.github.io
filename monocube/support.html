Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection?
No. Monocube works fully offline.

Are there ads or purchases?
No. Monocube 1.0 is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

How do I play?
Tap a block to slide it out in the direction of its arrow. A block can leave only if nothing is in its way. Drag to rotate the cube, pinch or scroll to zoom. Clear every block to finish the level.

I'm stuck.
Use a hint (light bulb) to highlight a free block, or the hammer to break one. Both can be bought with coins you earn by playing.

Where is my progress saved? Will it move to a new device?
Progress is saved only on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere, so it does not transfer automatically.

How do I start over?
Settings → Reset progress.
