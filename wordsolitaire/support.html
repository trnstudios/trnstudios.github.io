Solitaire Owl — Support

How to play

Place a crowned category card on an empty slot and drag the words of that category onto it. On the table, words of the same category stack on each other. Finish every category before you run out of moves.

FAQ

A card won't go where I drop it. Cards only go onto their own category, or onto a card of the same category on the table. A wrong drop slides back and does not use a move.

I ran out of moves. Open ☰ and choose "Restart" to replay the level with the same deal.

How do I change the language? ☰ → Settings → Language. Words and menus switch between English and Turkish.

Is my progress saved? Progress is stored on your device and kept until you delete the app.
