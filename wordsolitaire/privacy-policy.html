Solitaire Owl — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Solitaire Owl (TRN STUDIOS) does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Stored on your device

Your level progress, coins and helper counts, and your sound, music, haptics, language and motion settings are kept only on your device, in the app's own folder. They never leave your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Not collected

  • No account, name, email or contact details are requested.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • No ads, advertising identifier (IDFA), tracking, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • The app does not connect to the internet.
  • There are no in-app purchases.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from children either.
