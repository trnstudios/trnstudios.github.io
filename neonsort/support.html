Contact us

For problems, ideas or questions, email: trnstudios.support@gmail.com. In the game, Settings → Support → "Report a problem" copies a technical summary to your clipboard that you can paste into the email (it has no personal data: device model, version, level).

FAQ

Where is my progress saved? Only on your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress.

Are there ads or purchases? No. Version 1.0 is completely free; every skin and level is unlocked with in-game coins.

Sound / vibration / color-blind mode? Toggle them in Settings.
