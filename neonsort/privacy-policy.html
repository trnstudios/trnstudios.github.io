Neon Sort Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated: 17 November 2026 · TRN Studios (Mertcan TURAN)

In short

Neon Sort does not collect any personal data. The game has no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts and no tracking. App Store privacy label: "Data Not Collected".

Information that stays on your device

The game stores your progress (level, stars, in-game coins, chosen skin and settings) only in a save file on your device. This file is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the game deletes the file.

Third parties

This version uses no third-party services (ad networks, analytics, cloud save, game services) and does not connect to the internet. The privacy / support links in Settings open these pages in your device's browser.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone of any age.

Changes

If ads or purchases are added in a future version, this page and the store privacy label will be updated before that release.
